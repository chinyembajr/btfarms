About Buya-Ntashi Farms

Learn more about who we are and what we stand for.

Company Overview

Buya-Ntashi Farms is an urban farming initiative focused on potato farming and fish farming. The initiative promotes farming as a way of improving food security, generating income and creating employment opportunities.

We also aim to connect people with useful farming information, practical farming knowledge and opportunities within the agricultural sector.

Our Vision

To promote modern and sustainable farming that contributes to stronger communities and a better future.

Our Mission

Our mission is to promote potato and fish farming by sharing useful farming information, encouraging sustainable practices and creating opportunities for people to participate in agriculture.

Our Values

We encourage farming practices that support a better future.
