    About Fish Farming

    Fish farming involves raising fish in controlled environments for food production and commercial purposes.

    Fish Pond Management

    Proper feeding, water quality and pond maintenance help promote healthy fish growth.

    Feeding and Water Quality

    Fish need suitable feed and clean, well-managed water. Regularly checking pond conditions and feeding responsibly can support fish health and reduce waste.

    Harvesting and Benefits

    Harvesting at the right size helps provide a reliable source of food and can create income opportunities for farming households and local communities.
